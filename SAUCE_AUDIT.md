# CookTogether — sauce and accompaniment audit

**Review date:** 17 November 2026  
**Inventory:** 116 active recipes in `window.COOK_RECIPES` (the app's visible recipe data; not its separate legacy data).  
**Outcome:** 15 recipes with opt-in sauce/accompaniment (14 new, one already present), 101 recipes unchanged.  

## Principles

- Preserve original ingredients, cooking instructions, temperatures and timers. An optional sauce must not change the core recipe.
- A sauce is not an automatic improvement. Curries, stews, pasta with sauce, soups, saucy pies, glazed fish and dishes with a dressing/dip already have moisture; avoid competing flavours.
- For crispy food and roasting trays, prepare accompaniments separately to avoid steaming or softening it. Sauces are spooned on at service.
- Reserve clean marinade before raw meat contact. Never reuse marinade from raw meat/fish unless cooked thoroughly. Handle chicken and fish safely and do independent doneness checks.
- Opt-in extras appear in Get Ready, a cook-along action near serving, recipe details and a manually activated **Add sauce extras to Shopping** option. Shopping/Pantry are not silently changed by choosing a sauce. Check ingredients for allergens, dietary requirements and salt content.
- All new sauce formulations are **development proposals, NOT kitchen tested**. Published pairing methods inform the combinations; neither sources nor this audit validate exact ingredient quantities or cooking results.

## Testing and remaining work

- Run `node --check optional-sauces.js` and `node tests/optional-sauces.cjs`.
- Run existing `node tests/traybake-timers.cjs`, `node tests/task-illustrations.cjs` and `node tests/illustrations.cjs`.
- Do a physical mobile browser QA on Get Ready, cook-along step order, scaled serving quantities, manual Shopping extras and save/resume.
- Test each sauce in a real kitchen before calling it 'kitchen-tested'. Especially check saltiness for soy/teriyaki, thickening of gravies and how the extra prep affects the cook's ability to follow the main recipe.
- Optional shopping ingredients persist as separate manual shopping items after selection. If the sauce is deselected, the user can delete these from Shopping as with other extras.
