📱 Add CookTogether to your home screen

Samsung / Android (Chrome)

  1. Open CookTogether in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen (or Install app, if offered).
  4. Confirm Add or Install.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open CookTogether in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

This currently adds a handy home-screen shortcut. We're working towards a fully installable app.
